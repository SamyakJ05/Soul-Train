The idea behind Soul Train

Playlists should have an arc, not just a category.

Most mood playlists hold you in one place. Soul Train is designed for change: settling down after work, building toward a run, or finding your way out of a low-key afternoon.

Your choices lead

You control the moods, shape, era, length, discovery level and privacy. The model arranges the path.

Spotify stays Spotify

Authorization happens on Spotify. Soul Train never sees your password and only requests permissions needed to read saved tracks and create playlists.

Built for current APIs

Mood scoring uses the project's local dataset. Spotify is used for account connection, saved-library access, and playlist delivery.
